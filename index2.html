North Bellevue Community Center Advisory Board

Supporting community programs, seniors, families, and the arts

The North Bellevue Community Center offers an array of services for adults, especially seniors, and children of all ages. The center partners with community members to maintain independence, health, and well-being by providing support, referrals, activities, and learning opportunities.

Programs include health and wellness, cultural and performing arts, human services, drop-in activities, socialization, information and referrals, community dining, day trips and a wide array of fitness classes. The website for the center is bellevuewa.gov — North Bellevue Community Center.

Members of the advisory board volunteer to help with special events, fundraising activities, and assist with programming. The advisory board is a 501(c)(3) organization (EIN 94-3157035).

The center is located at: 4063 148th Avenue NE, Bellevue
The advisory board email contact: NBCCAdvisoryBoard@outlook.com
